Built for MQTT

See your broker conversation, not a wall of bytes.

Most proxies stop at HTTP. IoT and real-time apps don't. Point your app at a local port and Wiretide relays it to your broker, decoding every packet on the way through.

  • Plain TCP, TLS or WebSocket. For MQTT over TLS it terminates and re-encrypts, so you see the packets without changing the broker.
  • A live topic tree. Browse home/kitchen/temperature the way you think about it, with the last payload, message rate and counts per topic.
  • Charts from your payloads. Pick a numeric JSON field and watch it change over the session.
  • Session context. Client ID, protocol version, keep alive and per-direction topic aliases are tracked per connection.

Pro tools

Don't just watch the traffic. Change it.

Test the edge cases your backend can't give you on demand: a slow response, a missing field, a broker that sends 41.5 °C. Pro works on HTTP and MQTT alike.

Breakpoints

Pause a request before it leaves, or a response before your app sees it.

Edit the method, URL, headers, status or body in place, then continue, return your edit or abort.

  • Match by host, path and method, on requests, responses or both
  • JSON editor with formatting and validation
  • Edited exchanges are marked in the timeline
Map & Rewrite

Mock an endpoint, point it at localhost, or fix a header on the fly.

Rules apply live as traffic flows, with no restart and no code changes in your app.

  • Map Local answers from an inline body or a file
  • Map Remote sends matching requests to another server
  • Rewrite URLs, headers, query, status, body and JSON values
  • MQTT rules rewrite topics, payloads and retain flags over TCP, TLS and WebSockets
MQTT publish & replay

Send any message to your broker, or replay one you captured.

Pick a topic, QoS, retain flag and MQTT 5 properties, then send once or on a repeat to test how your app copes.

  • Replay a captured PUBLISH with one click
  • JSON, text or hex payloads
  • Send N times at a fixed interval
Scripting

When a rule isn't enough, write a little JavaScript.

Define onRequest, onResponse or onPublish to edit traffic, or answer locally with respond().

  • Highlighted editor with a live syntax check and templates
  • Console output, errors and a trial run on captured traffic
  • Runs on HTTP, MQTT and MQTT over WebSockets

FAQ

Questions

Does my traffic leave my Mac?

No. Captures are stored locally in a SQLite database on your Mac, and scripts run on your Mac too. The only network calls the app makes on its own are checking your Pro license with the store and checking for updates.

What's free and what needs Pro?

Capturing and inspecting everything is free: HTTP, HTTPS, WebSocket and MQTT, search, timing and export. Pro adds the tools that change traffic (breakpoints, map and rewrite, MQTT publish and replay, scripting) and keeps unlimited saved sessions.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

A debugging proxy needs to run a local proxy, install a root certificate and talk to the iOS Simulator, which the App Store sandbox doesn't allow. Wiretide is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, and it updates itself.

How do I capture MQTT?

Add your broker in Settings. Wiretide listens on a local port, for example localhost:18830, and forwards to the broker. Point your app at that port. MQTT over WebSocket is picked up automatically through the HTTP proxy, and rules and scripts apply there too.

Does it work with the iOS Simulator and real devices?

Yes. The Simulator uses your Mac's proxy settings, and Settings can install the root certificate into a booted Simulator for you. For a physical device, set the Wi-Fi proxy to your Mac and trust the certificate on the device.

What happens after my 12 months of updates?

Pro keeps working forever on every version released within 12 months of your purchase. Renew if you want the newer ones. A license also keeps working offline for 30 days between checks.
