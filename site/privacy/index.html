Privacy Policy

Last updated November 4, 2026

The short version: the traffic you capture never leaves your Mac. Wiretide has no analytics, no tracking and no ads. It only goes online to look for updates and to check your Pro license.

Who we are

Wiretide is made by [LEGAL NAME] ("we", "us"). If you have a question about your data, email hello@wiretide.app.

The traffic you capture

Everything Wiretide captures, including requests, responses, MQTT messages, saved sessions, rules and scripts, is stored only on your Mac. We never receive it, and nothing in the app uploads it. The root certificate Wiretide creates to inspect HTTPS is generated on your Mac and its private key stays there.

What the app sends over the internet

  • Update checks. About once a day, Wiretide downloads a small list of available versions from wiretide.app. Like any web request, this includes your IP address and the app and macOS version.
  • Pro license checks. When you activate Pro, Wiretide sends your license key and your Mac's name to Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, so the activation shows up in your account and you can manage it there. Every few days it re-checks the key with Lemon Squeezy. A license keeps working offline for up to 30 days between checks.
  • Downloads. New versions are downloaded from GitHub, which receives your IP address as part of the download.

The free version sends nothing except update checks. Wiretide has no crash reporting, usage statistics or advertising identifiers.

Purchases

Wiretide Pro is sold through Lemon Squeezy, which acts as the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy collects your name, email, billing address and payment details to process your order, issue receipts and handle taxes. We receive your name, email, country and order details so we can send your license and help with support. We never see your full card details. See the Lemon Squeezy privacy policy for how they handle your data.

This website

wiretide.app uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes basic request data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. If you join the release mailing list, your email is stored with our newsletter provider and used only to tell you about Wiretide releases. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Emails you send us

When you email us, we keep the conversation so we can help you. We don't share it or use it for marketing.

How long we keep data

We keep order and license records for as long as your license exists and as required for tax and accounting. Support emails are kept for as long as they are useful for helping you. Mailing list addresses are deleted when you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you, or to stop using it, by emailing hello@wiretide.app. Data held by Lemon Squeezy about your purchase can also be managed through them. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll say so in the release notes.
