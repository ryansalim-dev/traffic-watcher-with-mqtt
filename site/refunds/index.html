The short version: if Wiretide Pro isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of buying and you'll get your money back. No questions asked.

Try it first

Capturing and inspecting traffic is free, so you can make sure Wiretide works with your apps, devices and brokers before you buy Pro.

14-day money-back guarantee

You can get a full refund of your Wiretide Pro purchase, including renewals, within 14 days of the order date, for any reason.

How to ask for a refund

Email hello@wiretide.app with the email address you used to buy, or your order number from the receipt. You can also reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt or ask Lemon Squeezy directly. We aim to reply within 2 business days.

What happens next

Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller, to the original payment method. They usually appear within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank. After 14 days

After 14 days, purchases aren't usually refundable. If something went wrong, such as a duplicate order or a problem we couldn't fix, email us anyway and we'll do our best to make it right. This doesn't affect any rights you have under consumer protection laws where you live.
