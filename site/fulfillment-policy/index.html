Fulfillment Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026

This page explains how Wiretide Pro is delivered, and how refunds, returns and cancellations work. Purchases are processed by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record.

Refund Policy

  • All Wiretide Pro purchases, including renewals, come with a 7-day money-back guarantee, for any reason.
  • To request a refund, email hello@wiretide.app with the email address you bought with or your order number.
  • Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy to your original payment method, and usually appear within 5 to 10 business days.
  • Once refunded, the license key is deactivated and the app goes back to the free version. Your captured traffic and saved sessions stay on your Mac.

Delivery Policy

  • Wiretide Pro is a digital product. Your license key and download link are emailed to you right after purchase.
  • The license is perpetual and includes 12 months of updates.
  • It is a one-time payment, with no recurring charges and no automatic renewal.

Return Policy

  • As a digital product, Wiretide Pro can't be physically returned. Refunds are handled under the Refund Policy above.
  • If something isn't working, please email us first. Capturing and inspecting traffic is free, so you can also check that Wiretide works with your setup before you buy.

Cancellation Policy

  • Wiretide Pro is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. To undo a purchase, request a refund within 7 days.
  • For duplicate orders, team purchases or other special cases, email hello@wiretide.app and we'll sort it out.

Contact

Questions about an order go to hello@wiretide.app. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
